Lijiang

Ancient Town Under Snow Peaks · 2,400m

Day 4 — Blue Moon Valley + Baisha

Thu, Oct 29
8:00 AM — 12:30 PM · Blue Moon Valley + Spruce Meadow
Blue Moon Valley — turquoise glacial pools at foot of Jade Dragon Snow Mountain
RM 82/person (entrance + shuttle bus)

Blue Moon Valley + Spruce Meadow (蓝月谷 + 云杉坪) — The highlight of Lijiang! Turquoise glacial lakes with white waterfalls. The color comes from copper ions and limestone. Shuttle bus — no hiking needed. Spruce Meadow accessible by cable car for alpine views.

⚠️ At ~3,100m altitude — limit to 2-3 hours. Bring sunscreen, warm layers, water. Buy portable oxygen canisters at entrance. Watch babies closely.
2:00 PM — 4:30 PM · Baisha Ancient Town
Baisha Ancient Town — quiet Naxi heritage village

Baisha Ancient Town (白沙古镇) — Oldest of Lijiang's ancient towns. Famous Baisha Murals (600 years old). Quiet cobblestone streets, Naxi houses, tie-dye workshops. Stunning Jade Dragon Snow Mountain views from main street.

💎 Glacial valley🌲 Alpine meadow🖼️ Ancient murals

Day 5 — Black Dragon Pool + Old Town + Lashihai

Fri, Oct 30
8:00 — 9:30 AM · Black Dragon Pool
Black Dragon Pool — Jade Dragon Snow Mountain reflected in crystal-clear water
RM 50/person

Black Dragon Pool (黑龙潭) — Crystal pool reflecting Jade Dragon Snow Mountain. Red pavilion, stone bridges, ancient trees. Flat, stroller-friendly. Koi fish. Best in morning light!

9:30 AM — 1:00 PM · Lijiang Ancient Town
Lijiang Ancient Town — UNESCO World Heritage, canals and Naxi culture

Lijiang Ancient Town (丽江古城) — UNESCO site. Canals, giant water wheels, Mu's Residence. Sifang Street, Naxi handicrafts, yak yogurt, fried Baba bread. 4-year-old will love the waterwheels!

2:00 — 3:00 PM · Travel to Lashihai
3:00 — 6:00 PM · Lashihai Wetland
Lashihai Wetland — Yunnan's first nature reserve with migratory birds

Lashihai Wetland (拉市海湿地公园) — Bird Watching Corridor, boardwalks, Swan Lake, Children's Playground. Late Oct = bird migration from Siberia! Boat rides, horse riding. Stroller-accessible.

6:00 — 6:45 PM: Return to hotel.

🪞 Mirror pool🏘️ UNESCO town🐦 Wetland birds

Practical Tips

🚂 Transport

  • Book trains via Trip.com or 12306
  • Under-1.2m children ride free
  • Private van within cities (~RM 200-300/day)
  • Apps: Didi, Alipay, WeChat, Trip.com

🎒 Packing

  • Baby carriers > strollers at most spots
  • Warm layers (Lijiang nights: 4-10°C)
  • Rain jacket, sunscreen + hats
  • Wet wipes, hand sanitizer

🍜 Food

  • Crossing-the-bridge noodles (过桥米线)
  • Erkuai rice cakes, yak yogurt
  • Fresh pomegranates, mandarins
  • Halal: look for 清真 signs

🚨 Emergency

  • China: 120 (medical), 110 (police)
  • MY Embassy: +86-10-6532 2531
  • 30-day visa-free for MY passport
  • Keep hotel name card in Chinese!
